# Slide illustrations

The pictures in this folder were generated with DALL-E (OpenAI) from the prompts below, in October 2026, and converted to JPEG, 960 px wide at quality 80 (`sips -Z 960 -s format jpeg -s formatOptions 80`). The original PNGs (about 1 MB each) are not in the repository; the author keeps them locally. The licence terms are those of the generating service; check them before reuse.

| File | Used in | Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| `dino-whiteboard.jpg` | `slides-fizzbuzz.md` | Flat vector illustration of a friendly green dinosaur teaching at a whiteboard, simple geometric shapes, limited palette of teal, orange and off-white, no text, transparent-looking clean background, 16:9. |
| `test-pyramid-platforms.jpg` | `slides-test-strategy.md` | Minimal isometric illustration of three stacked platforms of increasing size labelled by colour only (small top, medium middle, wide base), soft shadows, pastel palette, no text. |
| `red-green-refactor.jpg` | `slides-fizzbuzz.md` | Simple line illustration of a loop of three arrows around a laptop, one red, one green, one blue, flat style, white background, no text. |
| `student-teacher.jpg` | `slides-test-strategy.md` | A student and a teacher looking at a screen each: one showing a lesson page, one showing slides (a less childlike variant of the first prompt). The exact prompt was not recorded. |
