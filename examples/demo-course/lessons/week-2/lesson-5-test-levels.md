# Lesson 2.5: Test levels

An overview page: the slides of this lesson contain the slides of the three level pages below as nested sets.

## 1. Why levels

- Each level of test answers a different question.
- A cheap test at a low level finds a fault sooner than an expensive test at a high level.
- Together the levels form the test pyramid.

## 2. The levels

- [Unit tests](levels/level-unit.md)
- [Integration tests](levels/level-integration.md)
- [End-to-end tests](levels/level-end-to-end.md)

## 3. Choosing a level

- Test a rule at the lowest level that can show it.
- Test a connection between parts at the integration level.
- Test a whole journey of a user at the end-to-end level, and only a few of them.
