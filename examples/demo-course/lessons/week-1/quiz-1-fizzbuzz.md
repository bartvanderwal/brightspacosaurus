# Quiz 1: FizzBuzz

## Question 1

What does FizzBuzz print for the number 15?

- A. Fizz
- B. Buzz
- C. FizzBuzz
- D. 15

Correct answer: **C**

## Question 2

What does FizzBuzz print for the number 7?

- A. Fizz
- B. Buzz
- C. FizzBuzz
- D. 7

Correct answer: **D**

## Question 3

Which numbers produce "Fizz" (divisible by 3 but not 5)?

- A. 3, 6, 9, 12
- B. 5, 10, 20, 25
- C. 15, 30, 45
- D. 1, 2, 4, 7

Correct answer: **A**
